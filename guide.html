Family Dinner Planner — How It Works

Plan the week's dinners together, let everyone vote, and never stare into the fridge wondering what to make.

Open the app →

🚀 Getting started (one person does this once)

  1. Open the app and tap “New here? Create your family →”.
  2. Enter your invite code (from whoever invited you), then choose an email and password for your whole family to share — everyone signs in with these same two things, so pick a password you're comfortable telling your kids.
  3. List who's eating (e.g. Maya, Sam, Ezra) — these become the names everyone rates under.
  4. Tell it what your family eats: which proteins are on the table, your dairy comfort level, kosher-style, healthy focus. This shapes your starter recipes and everything the app suggests later. (All of this is changeable anytime in ⚙️ Settings.)
  5. Tap Create family — you'll land in the app with a starter library of dinners matched to your food profile.

📱 Get everyone on board

Each family member, on their own phone:

  1. Opens the same link and signs in with the shared family email + password (just once — the phone remembers).
  2. Picks who they are when asked — that's how their 👍s and ⭐s get counted.
  3. Adds it to the home screen so it feels like a real app:
    iPhone: in Safari, tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen.
    Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or “Install app”).

Everything syncs live — a rating on one phone shows up on all the others.

➕ Adding dinners (the fun part)

Tap 📚 Library → ✨ Add dinners. You've got three ways:

✨ Generate — including “what's in my fridge?”

Pick any criteria (protein, prep time, cuisine) and tap Generate ideas. The magic box is “Ingredients I have”: type what's actually in your fridge — chicken thighs, spinach, half a lemon, rice — and it invents dinners built around them. Tap ＋ on any idea you like; it lands in your Library complete with ingredients and a recipe.

🔗 From a link

Found a recipe online — a food blog, NYT Cooking, grandma's synagogue newsletter posted to the web? Paste the link and tap Import recipe. The app pulls out the name, ingredients, and steps for you to review before adding. No more retyping recipes.

✍️ Add your own

For family classics that live in your head. Type a name and ingredients — and if you want, let the AI helper draft the recipe steps from your ingredients (or the reverse: paste your steps and it writes the shopping list).

🗓️ The weekly rhythm

  1. Everyone votes ahead of time. In the 📚 Library, each person taps 👍 / 👎 on meals — “sounds good” or “not for me.” This is how kids get a real say without a dinner-table debate.
  2. Get a suggested plan. On 🍽️ Plan, tap 🔄 Suggest. The app proposes dinners weighted by your family's ratings — favorites show up more, recent repeats are held back, 👎 meals fade away. Swap any single meal, or swap them all.
  3. Assign days & approve. Give each dinner a day, then tap ✓ Approve. You get a calendar file (dinners appear on your phone calendar at 6 PM) and a consolidated shopping list.
  4. Shop. Uncheck what you already have, then send the list to Instacart or copy it wherever you shop.
  5. Cook & mark it made. Recipes are right on each meal card. Afterward, tap Mark as made.
  6. Rate after eating. On ✅ Cooked, everyone gives 1–5 ⭐. Ratings feed the next week's suggestions — the app literally learns what your family loves.
💡 The secret to good suggestions: the more your family taps 👍/👎 and ⭐s, the smarter the plan gets. Hand your kid the phone after dinner and let them rate — it takes ten seconds and it's their favorite part.

🧑‍🍳 Little extras worth knowing

  • Cook notes: after making a dish, jot a note (“needed 10 more minutes,” “kids wanted it less spicy”) on the Cooked tab — and let the AI revise the recipe from your note so next time it's already fixed. Old versions are kept.
  • ⭐ Favorites: on the Library tab, a dropdown of your family's top-rated dinners for quick re-adding.
  • 📊 Ratings tab: see everyone's votes side by side — settle the “I never liked that!” arguments with data.
  • ⚙️ Settings: rename your family, add/remove members, change how many dinners get planned at a time, and update your food profile as tastes change.

❓ FAQ

Does it cost anything?
No — it's free for invited families.
Can other families see our meals or ratings?
No. Each family's data is completely private to their own account.
We forgot our password.
On the sign-in screen, enter your family email and tap “Forgot password?” — a reset link goes to that email.
Someone rated under the wrong name.
Tap the “You: …” button at the top to switch who the device belongs to. Any rating can be changed by tapping it again.
Can two dinners go on the same night, or a night be skipped?
Sure — assign days however you like; the app just flags doubled-up days so it's a choice, not an accident.
